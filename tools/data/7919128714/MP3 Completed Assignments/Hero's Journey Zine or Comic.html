Hero's Journey Zine or Comic

Due: Friday, January 23, 2026 at 11:59 pm · Locks on Monday, January 5, 2026 at 2:43 pm
Score: 105/100

Description / Directions

Select one:

  • After learning about the hero’s journey, and reading “The Cyclops” from The Odyssey, consider where you have encountered the hero’s journey in a story that you have read or movie you have seen. In a 7 paneled ZINE, retell a story that you know in words and pictures, labeling seven or more steps from the 12-step hero’s journey model. Then, answer the ECR question about fate and free will in the tale of the Cyclops on the back of the page.
  • After learning about the hero's journey, and reading "The Cyclops" from The Odyssey, retell the story of Odysseus's mini hero's journey in the tale. In five panels of a COMIC, recount the story in words and pictures, making sure to label five or more steps from the 12-step hero's journey model. Then, answer the ECR question about fate and free will on the handout.

Paper handouts given in class. 
Grading for this assignment is up to a 95. 10 extra points can be earned by reciting "Invictus" by William Ernest Henley for a total possible grade of 105.
