The Odyssey Research Project & Presentation

 

Essential Question: How does one aspect of the hero’s journey in The Odyssey relate to a modern-day hero story, when we attend to clues embedded in the text and contrast them with current news articles and biographical sources?

 

In pairs, create a presentation analyzing the hero’s journey in The Odyssey compared to a modern day hero. Your presentation should include the following requirements:

 

  1. Original title, image, student names, class period

  • 1 slide/page 

Formulate a response to the essential question. This should be an overview of your entire project, summarizing the synthesis of ideas. It is a 4-6 sentence introduction that compares the journeys of Odysseus and your hero at a high level. No text evidence is required.

  • Minimum 1 slide/page

Evaluate Homer’s depiction of Odysseus’ journey in the epic poem. Summarize each step of the hero’s journey. Then, analyze one aspect of the hero’s journey in detail. Explain the aspect in your own words; use and cite quotes/evidence from text to support your ideas.

  • Minimum 1 slide/page

Research and analyze a current news story for a modern day hero. Summarize each step of your modern person’s hero’s journey. Then, analyze one aspect of the hero’s journey in detail. Explain the aspect in your own words; use and cite quotes/evidence from text to support your ideas.

  • Minimum 1 slide/page

Comparative analysis paragraph: Analyze one step of the hero’s journey as it relates to Odysseus’s story and your modern person’s life. How do they compare? Incorporate facts, dates, evidence, and use MLA in-text citations. You may consider including an anecdote (personal experience or news story).

  • Minimum 2 sources/1 slide

Prepare 3 open-ended questions to facilitate class discussion about the topic.

  • 1 slide

Create a Works Cited Page using MLA format.

  • 1 slide

 

Remember to:

  • Make it visually appealing

  • Make it engaging to your audience

  • Divide the work equally 

  • Cite your sources4
