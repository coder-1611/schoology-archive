Music Video: Rubric

1. Create groups of 3-4. 

2. Choose a school appropriate song. 

3. Submit an email with the song lyrics. 

4. Everyone must have a role in the video. 

5. At least one video effect. 

6. At least one costume piece from home. 

7. At least one prop from home. 

8. Audience etiquette

9. Beginning, Middle and End (tell a story)

10. Characterization
