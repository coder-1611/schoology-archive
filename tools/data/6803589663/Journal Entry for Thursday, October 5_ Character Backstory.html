Roshambo: Character Backstory

Today you will be creating a backstory for your character. The more vivid your character is in your imagination, the easier it will be able to portray them on stage. This is a journal entry and must be a minimum of ten sentences. Below are possible questions to ask yourself as you create the backstory.

Possible Questions:

1. What is this person’s full name?

2. Where do they live?

3. When were they born? 

4. Where do they go to school?

5. What grade are they in?

6. What kind of grades do they get?

7. Are they involved in any other sports or activities?

8. Why do they play Roshambo? 

9. How would winning the tournament changer their life? 

10. How would you drescribe their relationship with their parents? 

11. Do they have any siblings?

12. What do they want to be when they grow up?

13. What kind of music do they listen to?

14. What kind of movies do they like?

15. What is their favorite food? Why?
