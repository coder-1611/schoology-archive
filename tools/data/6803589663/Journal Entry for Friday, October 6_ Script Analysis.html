Roshambo: Script Analysis

Using your script, you are going to answer the following questions. When the script does not explicitly come out and give you the answer to a question, you have the freedom to make an inference (a conclusion based on the information given). Answer all ten questions in complete sentences. This counts as a journal entry. 

1. Who is my biggest enemy in this play?

3. What are four physical traits of your character?

4. Who is one person your character trusts? Does that change at all by the end of the play?

5. Who is one person your character does not trust? Does that change by the end of the play?

6. By the end of the play, what does your character want?

7. What is your character’s biggest obstacle in this play?

8. What is one thing you and your character have in common?

9. What is the biggest way you and your character are different?

10. What makes your character different than any other character in the play?
