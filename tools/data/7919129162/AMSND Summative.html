Description / Directions

The Case Files:

After each reading, take some time to review your case files.  Add suspicious actions or words that could point to each suspect’s guilt. Write down the evidence, including Act, scene, and line #s, that support your ideas. Using complete sentences, explain how the evidence points to guilt.

 

The Trial (presentation):

On the day of the trial, your team will present your findings for the character most responsible for the chaos in the Athenian woods. Your presentation must include:

  • A visual presentation of your team’s findings, arranged in an interesting or creative way.

  • 1-2 minute presentation about who is responsible, why they are responsible, and text evidence that proves their culpability.

  • 1 minute presentations on why each of the other characters are NOT responsible.

    • Students should balance their presentation times equally.

    • Students may field questions from the jury (class).
