The Yellow Boat Post Read through questions

Due: Friday, November 22, 2024 at 11:59 pm
Score: 100/100

Description / Directions

Answer each question in complete sentences and use thorough detail. This is a more in depth journal entry than we've had all year; you're going to want to put in a sincere effort and dedicate some time to reflect on this story. This is also the last journal entry of the nine weeks, so if you do the math for grade calculations, an incomplete grade on this homework will really dent your grade!!

Huge tip: I encourage you to look back at the play as you complete your analysis. 

 

Story Analysis

1. in a complete paragraph, summarize David Saar's The Yellow Boat. Be sure to mention the elements of the story arc (exposition, moment of incidence, rising action, etc. )

2. Benjamin's characterization: what is Benjamin like? How would you define his character objectives in this play? (remember: all characters have motives, objectives, obstacles, and strategies in their own story)

3. What are Ben's parents like? What kind of family dynamics do they have with Benjamin?

4. Think about a few of the named characters played by the ensemble in the play. What role do they serve in Benjamin's Story? Name three characters, describe them, and talk about what they represent to Benjamin.

5. This play relies on an ensemble to build the different settings Benjamin finds himself in, creating an envornmental effect for Ben's school, community, and the world around him. List three of the groups the ensemble portrays in the show. 

Play Structure Analysis

5. This Play is told from the perspective and imaginative world of Benjamin. How does this affect how the characters act and speak?

6. In the preface of the show, the playwrite tells us that in this show, time is not linear. What does "cinematic time" mean, and how does that affect the flow of the play?

7. Benjamin is very creative, loves to draw, and loves to play! How does Benjamin use colors (literally and metaphorically) in the show? 

8. What purpose does the Yellow Boat serve in the story, both as a set piece, and an extended metaphor throughout the show?

Reflection

9. What were your favorite parts of this play? What made you laugh? What scenes were cool or interesting in your imagination during our read through? How did you feel from the start of the readthrough to the end of it?

10. What are your final takeaways from The Yellow Boat?
