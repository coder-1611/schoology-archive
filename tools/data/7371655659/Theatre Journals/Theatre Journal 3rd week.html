Description / Directions

Tell me about one of your favorite movies or books you’ve read. 

Write about the beginning of the story, noting the details of setting and character background of the exposition. Identify the moment of incidence in which the main character’s life changes forever. Describe the rising action that follows. Finally, write the climax of the story and the falling action / resolution that follows. Why does this story stand out to you?

 

As always, a paragraph with complete sentences is required. You may want to write a couple paragraphs as this is a more in-depth journal entry. HINT: a question exactly like this will be a big part of your first quiz, so try!!
