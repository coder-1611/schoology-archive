Description / Directions

1. Choose one of your poems you devised for class ( Passion, Loss, or Ignorance) and revise it. The goal of this revision is to expand on the ideas, metaphors, personal anecdotes, or word play you used in your chosen poem. Get more specific, find different perspectives, and craft a story that amplifies your voice! I want these poems to be at least two minutes in length Were you to read your poem aloud.

2. Write a brief pargraph explanating why you chose this poem to work with, and the inspiration you found while working on it / revising it.

 

To upload, ether post your poem in the Create tab of this assignment, or upload a picture of your revised poem if you prefer to hand write it. We will present these poems in class on Monday (2/24): I'll check to see if you've uploaded the poem that morning. 10 points off per day missing.
