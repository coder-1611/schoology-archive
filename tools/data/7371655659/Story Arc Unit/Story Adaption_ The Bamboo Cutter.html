Students will read the Japanese fairy tale, The Bamboo Cutter. They will mark the elements of this story's narrative arc: Exposition, Moment of incidence, Rising action, Climax, and Resolution that follows. 

Students will then be split into groups and begin adapting the story into a 5-7 minute performance that will be performed in front of the class. A brief analysis and justification of the group's adaption done individually up with will also be due in paragraph form.

 

Part One - As a Group, discuss the important moments of the story and brainstorm how to turn it into a show.

Part Two  - Create the story arc (beginning, middle, and end) of the ritual, write it down, brainstorm.

-Collaborate to establish team members theatrical roles in the performance. Everyone has a place at the table, and everyone has a voice. You are graded in part by your ability to work well with others. 

-Incorporate symbolism into your story and justify its use.

-Choreograph dynamic beats. Be specific!!

-Utilize body and movement to create a soundscape (the music and sounds of the world). You may use the lamp as a lighting practical if desired.

-At this time, brainstorm what costumes, props, mask decorations you’d like to use. 

Part Three - Rehearse and choreograph as a group: create stage pictures evocative of the story you aim to collectively tell. Incorporate levels and variations of movement, using your full acting instrument to story tell. 

Part Four - Design masks using paper plates and decorations, find costumes and props, and rehearse up to a performance ready level.

Part Five - final performance, class debrief and teacher notes, Post performance written analysis to follow.
