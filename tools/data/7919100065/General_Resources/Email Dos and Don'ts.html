I check my email during any school day from 8 am to 4:30 pm. If an email is sent in the evening or over the weekend, I will respond during the next school day. If you need quick information, it's often better to ask one of your classmates before asking me.

Examples of appropriate emails:
• I am sick and will miss the next 2 classes. What work will I miss?
• I will be absent next week for a family vacation next week during the test. When can I schedule a time to take it?
• Will you be available for tutorials Wednesday morning?
• This assignment says it's due on Friday but accepting submissions until Sunday. Can I get some clarification on this?

Examples of inappropriate emails:
• Can you update my grade in HAC? - If a grade is correct in Schoology but out-of-date in HAC, you can rest assured I will sync your grades in the next few days. At a minimum, I will sync grades on Tuesday mornings and after entering summative grades. There is no need to email me about this.
• What is wrong with my answer on this formative assessment? - We have time for formatives built into class each day. You may ask me for help during class or tutorials.
