Because of its location in the far East, China and other East Asian countries have a rich history and set of traditions. Their philosophies of Confucianism, Taoism, and Buddhism have shaped their society and government. Today they are rapidly growing economic and political powers and have global impact. Some countries have retained Communist systems, but others are adapting to more free market systems. Some of the countries are more economically developed and have more democratic processes (South Korea, Japan), while others are still challenged by dictatorships and oppression (China, North Korea).
