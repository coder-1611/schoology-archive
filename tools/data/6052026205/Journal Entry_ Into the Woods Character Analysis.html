Journal Entry: Character Analysis

  1. Where was your character right before they entered the scene? 

  2. Where is your character going next? 

  3. How old are they? 

  4. What is the thing that your character wants more than anything else? (Objective)

  5. How are they going to get what they want? (Motivation) 

  6. What will happen if they don’t get what they want? (Consequence)

  7. What is the social/economic status? 

  8. What is their educational level? 

  9. Who and/or what is their greatest obstacle? 

  10. Who is their greatest ally? 

  11. Summarize your character in a single adjective.
