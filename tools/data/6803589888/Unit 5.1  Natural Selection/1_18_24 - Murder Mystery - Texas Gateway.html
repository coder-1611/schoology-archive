1_18_24 - Murder Mystery - Texas Gateway

Description / Directions

Hello and welcome to the Texas Gateway Murder Mystery!

By now, you have used the first 2 of  the 4 E's from Texas Gateway located HERE to solve the murder of Mr. William DaVictim!!! (dun dun dun)

You have worked as a whole department, chief of police-led (teacher) group and then split off to work with your detective partner to solve the crime! You and your partner should have filled in the 'Gathering Evidence' document given to you by your chief of police (teacher). Then, you completed your police report (CER).

You will now work aside your partner to gather some CE (contining education) credits for your detective qualifications by working on the last 2 E's from Texas Gateway. Start with the 'Explain' and then work on the 'Explore'.

 

Good luck, and may justice prevail!!!
