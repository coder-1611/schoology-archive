Questions to consider and then answer at the end of the simulation:

  1. Upon signing your contract with the landlord, how did you expect the game to play out? Identify possible similarities and differences between the game and the reality of sharecropping.

  2. Based on your experience with the game, do you think sharecroppers had a reasonable opportunity to better their situation? Justify your position.

  3. Do you think the landlord’s power over you is fair? What do you think it means for someone to have a “fair” chance to achieve economic success?

  4. Compare and contrast sharecropping with slavery as a form of economic organization.

  5. Assess sharecropping as a form of agricultural organization. What are the advantages and disadvantages of the system? Why was this system created and put into practice?
