Divided Human Essay DUE 9_8

Description / Directions

Prompt: Choose one of the Native Texan tribes discussed in class (Karankawa, Caddo, Comanche, Jumano), and write 2 paragraphs describing their culture.  You may choose to be a reporter that is reporting about the tribe (expository essay), or you may choose to write a diary/ journal entries or a “day in the life” (personal narrative essay).  You must write your essay on notebook paper. You must include the following information in your essay:

  • Name of Tribe-

  • Culture Group--

  • Type of Shelter

  • Nomadic or Sedentary-

  • 2 Examples of food, and how they get their food (hunter-gatherers, farmer, both)--

  • At least 2 examples of general culture traits discussed in class (clothing, ceremonies, unique traits, etc.)

  • Description of the physical geography of the region they live
