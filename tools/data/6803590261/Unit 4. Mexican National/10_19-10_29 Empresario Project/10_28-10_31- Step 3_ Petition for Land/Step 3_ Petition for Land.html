Step 3_ Petition for Land

Description / Directions

Write a petition to the Mexican government to allow you to bring colonists to Texas.



Petition for Land Grant

Now that you know where you want your colony to be, you need to write a petition to the Mexican government.  Your petition must include:

  • What is your name, where are you from, what did you do before/ your previous job?

  • What characteristics do you have that would make you a good empresario? Ex: Leadership Skills, Communication Skills, Knowledge of Mexico & Laws

  • Who are you bringing with you? What types of people will you have in your colony? How will they benefit the colony and Mexico?  What qualities will your colonists have?

  • Where is your colony located?(Be specific. What rivers, lakes, and natural landmarks is it near?)  Why did you choose that location? (Refer back to Step 2)

  • How will your colony benefit Mexico?

 

Remember, you need to convince the Mexican government that they should grant your petition. Be sure to get approval from the Mexican government before you continue to the next step.
