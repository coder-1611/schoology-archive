Step 4_ Colony Ad

Description / Directions

Create an advertisement that would make someone want to leave their perfectly good situation to head to Texas. The advertisement must be easy to read, straight to the point, and grab the reader's attention.

Keep in mind that you are asking a person skilled in an occupation to come somewhere new. Be sure to refer back to the Colonization Law and your notes for information.

You may complete this by hand or electronically. Electronic projects must be linked to this document or attached in Schoology.

Include:
Land Policy for colonists
Requirements of colonists
Description of Land in Texas
Location of your colony
Cost of land & payment for colonists
